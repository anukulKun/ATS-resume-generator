# Story Bank

Use this file to collect interview stories from your own experience.

Keep 5 to 10 strong stories that you can reuse for common interview questions.

## Story Template

```text
### Story title
Source: Where this story came from, such as a job, project, course, or volunteer role

Situation: What was happening?
Task: What were you responsible for?
Action: What did you personally do?
Result: What changed because of your work?
Reflection: What did you learn?
Best for questions about: teamwork, conflict, leadership, problem solving, impact, etc.
```
